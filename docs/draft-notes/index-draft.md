### What is Civic AI?
Civic AI is a growing movement focused on deploying artificial intelligence responsibly within public service and governance through a resident-first lens. Rather than acting as a top-down authority or centralized control mechanism, it integrates into collaborative digital governance to build community-owned systems that protect democratic structures.

### 🧩 Core Principles of the Civic AI in Town

The initiative relies on six foundational concepts:
#### Broad Listening (Listening at Scale): 
  Using local AI to map community opinions simultaneously and give voice to quiet participants.
#### Plurality (Collaborative Diversity): 
  Finding rough consensus across diverse groups to bridge social divides instead of relying on binary votes.
#### Data as Soil (Regenerative Governance): 
  Treating community data as a protected local asset rather than an extractive commodity.
#### Digital Literacy: 
  Providing residents with a basic understanding of algorithms and data flow.
#### Digital Self-Determination: 
  Empowering communities to build and modify technology according to their own ethical values.
#### Digital Democracy: 
  Employing transparent digital platforms for direct resident involvement in local planning and budgeting.
